Meet the author

Faith belongs in the middle of real life.

Life rarely follows a tidy outline. We face unexpected storms, complicated relationships, unanswered questions, and days when we need a fresh reminder of hope.

I'm Billy Ready, Jr., a Christian author, pastor, educator, songwriter, and counselor. Through decades of ministry and counseling, I've seen how biblical truth speaks into ordinary struggles and life-changing seasons alike.

My writing brings Scripture into everyday conversations with warmth, practical insight, and an occasional bit of dry humor. My hope is to help you take the next faithful step, one day at a time.

Faithfulness in the ordinary
